# TRAZO
### Architecture, Scale Models & Design Objects

**Every great space begins with a line.**

TRAZO is a design-driven concept store dedicated to the art of architecture, physical model-making, and spatial exploration. We bring together the tools, materials, and precision objects that help architects, students, designers, and creative minds transform ideas into tangible forms.

From the first pencil stroke to the final miniature structure, TRAZO celebrates the process behind every great design.

---

## ◼ The TRAZO Philosophy

Architecture is more than the buildings we inhabit. It is the relationship between proportion, light, material, geometry, and the way people experience space.

TRAZO was conceived around a simple principle: **the process of creating deserves the same attention as the finished result.**

Our world combines technical precision with creative experimentation. Every drawing tool, modeling material, architectural miniature, and design object belongs to a broader creative journey — from observation and research to construction and presentation.

We believe in thoughtful design, functional tools, and the value of making ideas visible.

## 02 — Our Design Language

TRAZO follows an architectural visual language inspired by technical drawings, physical materials, and the geometry of built environments.

**Visual identity**

- **Graphite:** precision, structure, and technical clarity.
- **Warm ivory:** drawing paper, architectural models, and studio light.
- **Terracotta:** materiality, human warmth, and a distinctive creative accent.
- **Concrete gray:** the texture and honesty of architectural materials.

**Art direction**

The visual experience combines editorial typography, measured spacing, geometric compositions, material-inspired textures, and carefully framed product photography.

Rather than relying on conventional retail templates, TRAZO aims to feel like an independent architectural design studio with a thoughtfully curated collection.

## 03 — The Experience

The TRAZO storefront is planned around the way creative professionals discover and evaluate tools.

- **A considered homepage:** a distinctive introduction to the brand and its design philosophy.
- **Visual collections:** clearly differentiated categories with relevant imagery.
- **Product discovery:** search, category filters, and sorting options.
- **Detailed product presentation:** individual photographs, descriptions, prices, and relevant specifications.
- **A functional shopping bag:** quantity controls and subtotal calculation.
- **Studio journal:** educational articles, material guides, and model-making inspiration.
- **Customer support:** a dedicated contact area for questions about products and compatibility.
- **Responsive layouts:** an experience designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

The initial storefront is a static website concept. Payment processing, inventory management, customer accounts, and order fulfillment require separate integration.

## 09 — GitHub Pages Deployment

1. Push the website files to the repository's `main` branch.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select the `main` branch and the root directory as the publishing source.
4. Save the configuration.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site.
6. Test navigation, image paths, responsive layouts, and interactions on the published URL.

`https://bourdeau-be.github.io/trazo-arquitectura/`

## 10 — Product Photography Standards

Visual quality is central to the TRAZO experience.

Every product should have its own correctly assigned image, with consistent framing and enough resolution to show relevant details.

Image guidelines:

- Use individual photographs rather than repeatedly reusing the same image.
- Keep products centered and fully visible within their image frames.
- Prefer clean, neutral backgrounds that complement the brand.
- Avoid embedded prices, interface elements, watermarks, and unrelated objects.
- Use original photographs or assets with appropriate commercial licensing.
- Optimize images for web delivery without sacrificing important detail.
- Verify image paths and assignments before publishing.

Architectural models should be photographed to communicate their proportions, materials, construction, and scale clearly.

## 12 — Quality Assurance

A polished appearance is only one part of a reliable storefront.

Before each release, the project should be checked for:

- Broken image and stylesheet references
- Incorrect product-to-image assignments
- Missing files and invalid navigation links
- Responsive layout issues
- Search, filter, sorting, and shopping bag behavior
- Console errors and JavaScript failures
- Image file sizes and loading performance
- Accessibility basics, including keyboard navigation and readable contrast
- Correct behavior on the actual GitHub Pages deployment

A ZIP should be tested for archive integrity and its extracted file structure before delivery.
